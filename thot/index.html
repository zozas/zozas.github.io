Game Overview

In THOT, players face each other as rival alchemists striving to survive and eliminate their opponents. By combining alchemical substance cards, players generate powerful offensive or defensive effects. Designed for 2 to 8 players (individuals or balanced teams), games last approximately 10 to 15 minutes of tactical depth.

Game Background & Introduction

The Egyptian God Thot (Thoth/Toth) is considered the equivalent of the Greek Hermes and the first alchemist, tasked with preparing divine alchemical substances. Historically, only initiated priests—thought to be sons of Thot—could practice this sacred art.

Preparation & Setup

  • Players: 2 to 8 players, playing individually or in equal-sized teams.
  • Seating: Players sit in a closed circle. In team games, teammates cannot sit adjacent to each other.
  • Starting Stats: Every player begins with 7 Health Points (HP) and 0 Defense Points (DP) on their player sheet. Points cannot exceed 7.
  • The Deck: All 105 cards are shuffled. Each player is dealt exactly 7 cards to keep secret. The remaining deck rests in the center.

Battle Phase & Player Options

Play proceeds in clockwise rounds starting from the first player[cite: 2, 4]. On your turn, choose one of the following four actions:

  • Construct an Effect: Play 2 cards as ingredients to trigger a potion effect on self or an opponent. Played cards go to the bottom of the central deck. (Note: If you have only 1 card left, you may pass instead).
  • Pass: Take 1 card from the top of the deck.
  • Donate: Give 1 card from your hand to another player.
  • Special Trap: When attacked, defend instantly using a secret Special Card (even out of turn).

Damage, Death, & Winning

When damage is inflicted, it first reduces Defense Points until they reach zero, then reduces Health Points. When HP hits 0, the player is eliminated, returning all their cards to the bottom of the deck (or distributed as legacy in team games). The last surviving player or team wins the game.
